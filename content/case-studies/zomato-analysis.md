## Overview
An exploratory analysis of Zomato restaurant data focused on market trends and business insights.

## Problem
Restaurant stakeholders need a clear view of ratings, cuisines, prices, and location performance across a complex market.

## Approach / Solution
Validate the dataset, segment restaurants by meaningful attributes, and communicate patterns through focused visualizations.

## Key Features
- Market analysis
- Restaurant insights
- Data visualization

## Tech Stack
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Challenges & Solutions
- Compare categories with different sample sizes using grouped analysis.
- Separate useful signals from noisy or inconsistent rating data.

## Results & Learnings
- The analysis established a practical workflow for turning restaurant records into business questions.
- Review sentiment and live market monitoring are natural next steps.
