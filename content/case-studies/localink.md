## Overview
Localink is a local-discovery platform concept for finding useful services, places, and opportunities nearby.

## Problem
Local information is scattered across search results, social posts, and word of mouth, making trusted discovery harder.

## Approach / Solution
Combine location-aware browsing, useful categories, and clear provider information into one focused discovery flow.

## Key Features
- Location-based discovery
- Useful local categories
- Clear provider and place information

## Tech Stack
- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Location-aware UX

## Challenges & Solutions
- Keep local information accurate with provider verification and feedback.
- Use focused filters to support discovery without overwhelming visitors.

## Results & Learnings
- The concept clarified a simple path from local search to confident action.
- Maps, saved places, provider onboarding, and review controls are future directions.
