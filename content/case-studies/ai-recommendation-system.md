## Overview
An intelligent recommendation engine that personalizes content or product suggestions from behavior and item similarity.

## Problem
Users need relevant choices without manually searching through every available item.

## Approach / Solution
Combine collaborative filtering, content-based similarity, and contextual signals into a ranked recommendation workflow.

## Key Features
- Collaborative filtering
- Personalization
- Real-time recommendation direction

## Tech Stack
- Python
- Machine learning
- Recommendation systems
- Data science

## Challenges & Solutions
- Address cold starts with content signals and sensible defaults.
- Balance familiar recommendations with exploration of new items.

## Results & Learnings
- Combining multiple signals creates a more flexible recommendation foundation.
- A/B testing and real-time feedback are needed to measure useful engagement.
