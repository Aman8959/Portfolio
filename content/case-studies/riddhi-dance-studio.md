## Overview
A responsive website for a dance studio to present classes, instructors, facilities, and enrollment information.

## Problem
A studio needs an approachable online presence that turns interest into clear class inquiries and registrations.

## Approach / Solution
Organize class schedules, instructor profiles, gallery content, and booking actions into a mobile-friendly experience.

## Key Features
- Class showcase
- Instructor profiles
- Student portal direction

## Tech Stack
- Web development
- Responsive design
- Frontend development

## Challenges & Solutions
- Make schedules easy to scan across different screen sizes.
- Keep registration steps aligned with the studio's real operational process.

## Results & Learnings
- The site creates a clearer digital front door for prospective students.
- Membership plans, virtual classes, and progress tracking are useful future additions.
