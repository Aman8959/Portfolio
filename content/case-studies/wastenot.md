## Overview
WasteNot is a marketplace concept for helping rural entrepreneurs monetize resources and reduce waste.

## Problem
Distributed producers often lack accessible channels to reach buyers and coordinate sustainable commerce.

## Approach / Solution
Build seller onboarding, product discovery, transaction handling, and community trust features around a focused marketplace workflow.

## Key Features
- Marketplace
- Community engagement
- Resource optimization

## Tech Stack
- Full-stack development
- Web application
- E-commerce

## Challenges & Solutions
- Build trust with profiles, ratings, and transparent order history.
- Design payment and logistics flows for distributed sellers and buyers.

## Results & Learnings
- The platform establishes a practical foundation for local digital commerce.
- Seller growth, fulfillment quality, and access to financing are important next measures.
