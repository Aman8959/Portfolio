## Overview
Recall AI is a task, study, and project-management concept for helping people complete meaningful work.

## Problem
Important work can disappear inside overloaded task lists, repetitive reminders, and competing priorities.

## Approach / Solution
Combine behavior-aware recommendations with adaptive reminders and a Next Best Action score that considers urgency, progress, preferences, and notification fatigue.

## Key Features
- Behavior-aware recommendations
- Adaptive reminders
- Next Best Action scoring

## Tech Stack
- AI product design
- Recommendation systems
- Full-stack architecture

## Challenges & Solutions
- Reduce notification fatigue with user-controlled reminder intensity.
- Make recommendations understandable through clear reasons and feedback.

## Results & Learnings
- The concept clarified how useful AI should support action instead of adding more noise.
- Next steps are user testing, measurable completion signals, and careful personalization.
