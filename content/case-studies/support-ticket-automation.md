## Overview
An intelligent support-ticket workflow for classification, routing, and operational visibility.

## Problem
Support teams lose time when high-volume tickets are manually prioritized and assigned.

## Approach / Solution
Combine ticket ingestion, NLP-based categorization, routing rules, and agent-facing status and analytics views.

## Key Features
- Ticket automation
- Intelligent routing
- Priority classification

## Tech Stack
- Full-stack development
- AI and machine learning
- Automation
- Backend systems

## Challenges & Solutions
- Keep automation reviewable so agents can correct classifications.
- Scale routing decisions around team skills, workload, and ticket urgency.

## Results & Learnings
- The workflow creates a foundation for faster triage and clearer ownership.
- Verified production metrics and feedback-driven retraining are the next measurement steps.
