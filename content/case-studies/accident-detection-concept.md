## Overview
An AI-assisted emergency-response concept for detecting potential road accidents from CCTV and video streams.

## Problem
Manual monitoring can delay recognition and routing of possible road incidents.

## Approach / Solution
Explore computer-vision detection, temporal analysis, evidence capture, and a human-reviewable incident workflow.

## Key Features
- Video-stream monitoring
- Vehicle and person detection
- Incident workflow concept

## Tech Stack
- Computer vision
- OpenCV
- YOLO
- Deep learning

## Challenges & Solutions
- Treat alerts as reviewable signals rather than unquestioned decisions.
- Evaluate camera angle, weather, lighting, and location reliability before escalation.

## Results & Learnings
- The concept emphasizes responsible detection and traceable incident context.
- Measured performance evaluation and emergency-service integration are future work.
